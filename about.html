About CLM Consulting

An independent building consultancy practice built on professional integrity, technical rigour and genuine client care.

Who We Are

CLM Consulting is an independent building consultancy and quantity surveying practice based in Gloucestershire. We work with commercial clients, property owners, developers and businesses across the South West and wider UK, providing specialist advice at every stage of the property and construction lifecycle.

Our practice was established to offer clients something that larger firms often cannot: direct access to experienced professionals, genuinely independent advice, and a consistent standard of service from first instruction through to project close.

We are not aligned with any contractor, developer or funding body. Our advice is given solely in the interests of our clients — commercially sound, technically accurate and clearly communicated.

How We Work

  • Independence

    We have no contractor relationships, referral arrangements or commercial affiliations that could compromise our advice. Every instruction is approached with complete objectivity.

  • Technical Rigour

    Our work is underpinned by thorough analysis, accurate measurement and careful attention to contractual and technical detail. We do not cut corners.

  • Transparency

    We communicate clearly and honestly at every stage. Our clients receive straightforward advice, accurate reporting and no surprises.

  • Responsiveness

    We understand that property and construction matters are often time-sensitive. We are accessible, responsive and committed to meeting our clients' deadlines.

Our Expertise

Our team brings extensive experience across a broad range of project types and property sectors, including commercial offices, industrial and logistics, retail, mixed-use development, education, healthcare and residential. We have advised on projects ranging from single-property transactions to multi-million pound development programmes.

Professional Standards

Our work is conducted in accordance with the standards and guidance of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS). We maintain professional indemnity insurance and adhere to the RICS Rules of Conduct in all our professional activities.

  • RICS-Aligned Practice

    Work conducted to Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors standards and guidance.

  • Professional Indemnity

    Full professional indemnity insurance maintained on all instructions.

  • Gloucestershire Based

    Locally based with strong regional knowledge across the South West.

  • Independent Practice

    No contractor affiliations — advice given solely in the client's interest.

Where We Work

CLM Consulting is based in Gloucestershire and primarily serves clients across the South West of England, including Gloucestershire, Worcestershire, Oxfordshire, Wiltshire, Bristol and the surrounding counties. We also accept instructions from clients further afield where the nature of the project warrants it.
