About CLM Consulting

An independent building consultancy practice built on professional integrity, technical rigour and genuine client care.

Who We Are

CLM Consulting is an independent building consultancy and quantity surveying practice based in Gloucestershire.

We work with commercial clients, property owners, developers and businesses across the South West and wider UK, providing specialist advice throughout the property and construction lifecycle.

We are not aligned with any contractor, developer or funding body. Our advice is given solely in the interests of our clients.
