Employers Agent

Acting for you on a design-and-build project

An Employers Agent acts for you on a design-and-build project, administering the contract and protecting your interests from appointment through to completion. Final Accounts and Handover

Agreeing the true cost with chosen contractors

At final account and handover we agree the true cost of the works with the contractor, settling variations, claims and retention so the account is closed fairly and without loose ends. Insurance Rebuild Cost Assessments

Helping to access the cost of rebuilding

We asses the potential rebuild estimates, what it would cost to rebuild a property from scratch after a total loss, so the sum insured is accurate and correct. We inspect the building, measure and price the works to current rates, and provide a clear reinstatement figure you can use for insurance cover and periodic review.
