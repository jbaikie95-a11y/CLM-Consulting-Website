Our Services

A focused range of building consultancy and quantity surveying services delivered with professional integrity.

Specialist expertise across the full project lifecycle

From early-stage cost planning through to final account and post-completion matters, CLM Consulting provides the specialist advice and professional oversight that property owners, developers and businesses require. Each service is delivered with the same commitment to accuracy, independence and client care.

01

Quantity Surveying

Cost certainty from inception to completion

Effective cost management is fundamental to any successful construction project. We provide accurate, evidence-based cost advice at every stage — from initial feasibility estimates and detailed cost plans through to procurement strategy, tender analysis and final account settlement.

Scope of Service
  • Feasibility cost estimates and option appraisals
  • Detailed cost plans and elemental breakdowns
  • Procurement strategy and tender documentation
  • Tender analysis and contractor selection support
  • Interim valuations and payment certification
  • Variation assessment and change control
  • Final account negotiation and settlement
02

Project Management

Coordinated delivery from brief to handover

Successful project delivery requires clear leadership, rigorous programme management and effective coordination of all parties. We act as your representative throughout the project, managing consultants, contractors and stakeholders to keep delivery on time and on budget.

Scope of Service
  • Project brief development and feasibility
  • Consultant team assembly and management
  • Programme development and monitoring
  • Risk identification and management
  • Contractor procurement and appointment
  • Site progress monitoring and reporting
  • Handover and close-out coordination
03

Building Consultancy

Technical expertise for informed property decisions

Our building consultancy service provides specialist technical advice on building condition, defects analysis and property due diligence. Whether you are acquiring, disposing of or managing a property, we provide the clear, practical guidance needed to make informed decisions.

Scope of Service
  • Pre-acquisition technical due diligence
  • Building defect investigation and reporting
  • Planned maintenance programmes
  • Specification and tender for repair works
  • Party wall matters and neighbourly agreements
  • Insurance reinstatement cost assessments
  • Technical advisory for property transactions
04

Contract Administration

Impartial administration of building contracts

Proper contract administration protects all parties and ensures that building works are carried out in accordance with the contract. We act as contract administrator with complete impartiality — managing the contractual process, certifying payments, assessing variations and resolving disputes fairly.

Scope of Service
  • Contract preparation and execution
  • Pre-contract meetings and site establishment
  • Interim valuation and payment certification
  • Instruction and variation management
  • Programme and progress monitoring
  • Practical completion inspection and certification
  • Defects liability period management and final certificate
05

Dilapidations

Expert assessment and negotiation of dilapidations claims

Dilapidations disputes can represent significant financial exposure for both landlords and tenants. We provide expert assessment, well-evidenced schedules and skilled negotiation to protect your position — whether you are pursuing or defending a claim at lease expiry or during a tenancy.

Scope of Service
  • Schedules of dilapidations for landlords
  • Dilapidations responses and counter-schedules for tenants
  • Quantified demands and financial assessments
  • Supersession and diminution in value arguments
  • Negotiation and settlement of claims
  • Interim dilapidations advice during tenancy
  • Expert witness and dispute resolution support
06

Condition Surveys

Thorough inspection and clear, actionable reporting

A thorough condition survey provides an accurate picture of a building's state at a given point in time. Our surveys are methodical, clearly written and supported by photographic evidence — giving you the information needed to plan maintenance, manage risk or support a transaction.

Scope of Service
  • Pre-acquisition condition surveys
  • Schedules of condition for lease purposes
  • Planned preventative maintenance surveys
  • Reinstatement cost assessments
  • Drone-assisted roof and facade inspection
  • Photographic schedules and condition registers
  • Prioritised maintenance recommendations
